## Why a digital invitation beats a paper one

A custom wedding website does everything a paper invitation can't: guests RSVP instantly from their phone, you get a live guest count without chasing anyone down, and you can share event details, directions, registry links, and photos all in one place that updates in real time.

## What's included

Every wedding invitation site I build covers:

- **A designed landing page** matching your wedding's colors and theme — elegant, minimal, or bold, whatever fits you
- **RSVP form** with guest count, meal choice, and a note field — responses come straight to your email, no spreadsheet chasing required
- **Event details** — ceremony and reception times, venue address with a map link, dress code, and a schedule
- **Photo gallery** for engagement photos or a countdown to the big day
- **Mobile-first design** — most guests will open the link from a text message on their phone, so it has to look great there first

## How it works

1. **Tell me your date, theme, and colors** — even a Pinterest board or a couple of reference photos is enough to start
2. **I build a first draft** you can review and adjust — fonts, wording, layout
3. **RSVP data flows to your email** automatically as guests respond, so you always know your headcount
4. **Site goes live** on a simple link you can text, email, or put on a paper save-the-date

## Pricing

Because this is a smaller, faster build than a full business system, digital wedding invitation sites are priced lower and turned around quickly — usually within a week of getting your details, well ahead of most wedding planning timelines.
